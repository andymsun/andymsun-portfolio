lawvics.md Lawvics One legal question, answered across all 50 states. 2026 · shipped

Built in under 48 hours; finalist at the UChicago Vibe Coding Hackathon. One agent turns a question into fifty jurisdiction-specific searches and runs them in five batches. A second agent audits every result: schema checks, citation format, and a flag for statutes that may have been repealed. A map colors each state as its answer lands.

Next.js 16 · React 19 · TypeScript · Vercel AI SDK · Zod · Zustand · D3

Source Demo

rivendell.md Rivendell A live 3D map of everything moving around the planet. 2026 · shipped

8,000+ satellites, 6,000+ flights, and 500+ vessels drawn on Google's photorealistic 3D tiles, fed from OpenSky, CelesTrak, ADS-B Exchange, and AIS at once. The hard parts were on the client: thousands of moving objects at 60 fps, and a scene that stays readable at every zoom level.

Next.js · TypeScript · React · Three.js · Google 3D Tiles

sshfolio.go sshfolio This portfolio, as a program you ssh into. 2026 · active

A Go SSH server that gives every connection a terminal program instead of a shell: a prompt, slash commands, subagents, a coffee-cup mascot, and three swappable skins. No account, nothing to install. The terminal at the bottom of this window is a preview of it.

Go · bubbletea · wish · lipgloss · a €5 VPS

psiren.py pSiren Take a finished song apart and rebuild it with different voices. 2025 – · in progress

A music workspace for one person who wants the reach of a band. It separates a recording into vocal, instruments, and room, converts the lead with a voice model, and remixes over the original accompaniment, all on an Apple Silicon laptop. Next: per-instrument separation and instrument swaps that keep the notes and change the timbre.

Python · PyTorch (MPS) · Mel-Band Roformer · RVC

A biology final project that turned into a real game: a small roguelite dramatising a 2025 iScience study of moss spores exposed on the outside of the space station, with an in-game panel that cites the paper it came from.

TypeScript · Canvas · one iScience paper

cupboard.tsx Cupboard Companion A pantry and meal-plan hub that recognises what's in the photo. 2026 · shipped

About

I grew up in Flushing, Queens, and took the SHSAT without preparing. That worked out, and a few years later QuestBridge matched me to the University of Chicago, where I'm now a third-year studying computer science and computational & applied mathematics.

The through-line in everything here is human–computer interaction: making capable systems usable by people who did not build them. In practice that's been interfaces for language models that aren't a chat box, evaluation work where the uncertainty stays visible on the page, and terminal software, which is the most constrained interface there is and therefore the most honest one to design for.

Outside that: badminton at every open gym, music production, film photography, speedcubing, and an amount of coffee that my own portfolio makes jokes about.
